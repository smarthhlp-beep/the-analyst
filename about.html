About THE ANALYST

THE ANALYST is an AI-powered research tool that helps everyday Nigerian investors understand what a piece of news, a company filing, or a stock actually means for their money, in plain language, in seconds.

It started with a single piece of advice: a friend was guided to buy Wema Bank shares at ₦3–4 when few people were paying attention. Those shares have since risen roughly tenfold. That one decision led to a simple question: could this kind of guidance be made available to anyone, not just the lucky few who happen to know the right person?

Who it is for

THE ANALYST exists for the everyday investor: the person who wants to take part in moments like the Dangote IPO but doesn't know how to read a headline, a whitepaper, or a filing without paying for a personal account manager they can't afford, or trusting a "guru" who might be a scammer. We built it because too many people decide what to do with their money based on panic and hype instead of real analysis.

What makes it different

No course to buy, no account manager to hire, no guesswork. Paste a headline or a filing and get a clear, reasoned read, built around a structured research framework (connection, impact, action) instead of a generic chat box.
